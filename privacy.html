Privacy matters

This is an early version of NoHungryPets. We keep things minimal and transparent.

Privacy policy

NoHungryPets is a small, free, single-maintainer project. This page explains what information we collect, why, and who else sees it — in plain language, not legalese.

What we collect

  • When you create an account: your first name, email address, and postcode.
  • When you post a listing: the item details you enter (name, category, description, animal type) and any photos you upload.
  • When you message another user: the content of those messages, visible only to you and the person you're messaging.
  • If you email us directly: your email address and whatever you include in your message.

Your postcode is only ever used to show approximate distance and location on the map (down to postcode-area level) — your exact address is never required or stored.

Third-party services we use

As a free, no-budget project, we run on other companies' free tiers rather than our own servers. Each of these can see the data needed to do their specific job:

  • Firebase (Google) — hosts our sign-in, database (listings, profiles, messages), and vanity traffic statistics (Google Analytics). Google's own privacy policy applies to their side of this.
  • Firebase App Check / reCAPTCHA (Google) — runs quietly in the background to confirm requests are coming from a real browser, not a bot or script.
  • Cloudinary — hosts photos you upload with a listing.
  • postcodes.io — a free, open UK postcode-lookup service; the postcode/town you enter (e.g. when searching the map) is sent to them to get map coordinates back.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website itself.

Some pages also link out to Google Maps, PayPal, and our Facebook Page — once you click through, you're on their site under their own privacy policy, not ours.

Cookies & local storage

  • Strictly necessary: used to keep you signed in and remember basic preferences (like whether you've dismissed the install banner). These are essential for the site to function and don't require consent.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics (via Firebase) helps us understand how many people visit and roughly how they use the site. This is not strictly necessary and only runs with your consent — see the cookie banner shown on your first visit.

What we don't do

  • We never sell your personal data.
  • We don't add any tracking beyond what's listed above.

Deleting your data

Email us and we'll delete your account and associated data. We don't currently have a fully automated self-service delete button — for a project this size, a quick email gets it done just as fast and lets us make sure it's really you asking.

Questions or concerns? Email us at info.nohungrypets@gmail.com.
